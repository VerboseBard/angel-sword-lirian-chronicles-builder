2. Your token

First time: press Download Token Image, then drag the downloaded file onto the Owlbear map. When Owlbear asks what kind of image it is, choose Character (the sword). Your token is now on the map and saved in your Owlbear Characters list. Resize it if it is too big.

Later sessions: your token stays on the map in that scene. For a new scene, drag it in from your Owlbear Characters list, or press Place My Token and pick it (it is placed at one grid square).

Optional, also for GM repairs: link a token on the map to this character. Select exactly one token on the Character layer, then press Bind Selected Token. Rolls work without this.
