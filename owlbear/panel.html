2. Your token

One-time setup: download your token, then drag the file onto the Owlbear board. When Owlbear asks what kind of asset it is, choose the sword option — Character. Click the placed token and resize it with the arrows to the proper size. It now lives in your Characters library forever.

Every session after: enter the game through your link, press the button below, and pick your character.
